Before You Send It

Questions, Answered

Do I need off-road experience?

You need to be a confident road rider comfortable on a manual/semi-auto bike. Prior dirt experience helps hugely on the technical days (3–6), but we grade the route so beginners can take easier lines while advanced riders send the gnar. Total beginners should chat to us first.

What's the group size?

Maximum 8 riders per trip, plus a lead guide and a sweep rider — a 4:1 rider-to-crew ratio. Small groups mean we ride real trails, not convoy-crawl the highway.

What if I crash or the bike breaks?

It happens — it's off-road. Our chase truck carries a full spares kit and a spare bike. Guides are wilderness-first-aid certified and carry satellite comms for the dead zones. Travel insurance covering motorcycling is mandatory and we check it before departure.

When's the best time to ride?

We run trips Sept–May. Sept–Nov gives green rice terraces and firm trails; Dec–Feb is cooler and dry; Mar–May brings the flowers and warmer days. We avoid the peak monsoon so the river crossings stay fun, not fatal.

What licence do I need?

An International Driving Permit with a motorcycle endorsement is strongly recommended for legality and insurance. We'll send a full pre-trip pack once you book with everything you need to sort.

Can I come as a solo rider?

Absolutely — over half our riders come solo. You'll share homestay rooms (single supplement available) and leave with a crew of new mates. That's kind of the whole point.
